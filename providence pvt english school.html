Principal's Message

Welcome to Providence English Private School, where opportunity meets excellence! PEPS believes in creating an environment in which every student can thrive academically, socially, and personally. As a Principal, I am privileged to lead a committed team of educators dedicated to providing a transformative educational experience. Our goal is to foster a learning community that values diversity, promotes critical thinking, and prepares students for future challenges and opportunities and to become global citizens. We strive to foster an environment in which curiosity is sparked, creativity is encouraged, and innovation thrives. A commitment to high-quality learning is at the heart of our educational philosophy. We intend to offer a comprehensive curriculum that not only meets academic standards but also promotes holistic development. We enable our students to reach their full potential by combining rigorous academics, dynamic extracurricular activities, and a supportive network. Our school campus is purpose-built, complete with well-equipped classrooms, science laboratories, a library, a computer lab, playing fields, and sports halls. We host educational trips on a regular basis and participate in numerous inter-school competitions and events. We actively encourage our students to participate in a variety of activities to broaden their educational and personal experiences. We believe in the power of open communication and collaboration among students, parents, teachers, and the community. We work together to foster an environment that values respect, integrity, and a love of lifelong learning. I invite you to look around our website and learn about the many opportunities available at Providence English Private School. We welcome you to be a part of our vibrant learning community, whether you are a prospective student, parent, or community member. Thank you for taking an interest in PEPS.We are thrilled to be embarking on this educational adventure with you. Warm regards,

Marwa Diaa Principal,Providence English Private School
